Elide

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

In short: Elide does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything it does happens in your own browser, on your own computer.

What Elide does

Elide is a browser extension that lets you remove parts of a web page and adjust print settings before you print the page or save it as a PDF.

Information we collect

None. Elide has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. It never sends the pages you edit, your edits, or anything else about you to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your computer

Elide saves a few preferences, such as your paper size and margins, using your browser’s built-in extension storage. These stay on your computer and are deleted when you remove the extension. Your edits to a page are kept only while that tab is open.

Permissions and why Elide needs them

  • Active tab lets Elide work on the page you are viewing, and only after you click its icon or press its keyboard shortcut.
  • Scripting lets Elide add its editing tools to that page so you can select and hide parts of it.
  • Side panel shows Elide’s controls beside the page, so they never cover what you are printing.
  • Storage saves your print preferences on your computer.

Elide cannot read or change any page until you open it on that page.

Third parties

Elide does not sell, rent or share data, because it has none to share. It loads no code or content from outside the extension.

When you remove Elide, your browser opens a short, optional survey hosted by Tally asking why. It is not linked to you; the only detail passed along is Elide’s version number. Anything you choose to type is sent to Tally and read only to improve Elide.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. A change that collects any data would be explained here before it takes effect.
